About FitTrack

Your Personal Fitness Tracking Companion

Who We Are

FitTrack is a fitness tracking web application designed to help users monitor their daily workouts, track their fitness goals, and stay motivated throughout their fitness journey.

Whether your goal is weight loss, muscle gain, endurance improvement, or maintaining a healthy lifestyle, FitTrack helps you stay organized and focused.

What Our App Does

Users can register, log in, add exercises, monitor workout history, track progress, and view fitness statistics through an easy-to-use dashboard.

The system uses Local Storage to save workout records, making it simple and efficient without requiring a database.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage healthier lifestyles by providing a simple platform where users can track their workouts, measure progress, and achieve their fitness goals consistently.
